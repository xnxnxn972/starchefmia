A free restaurant menu game you can play in your browser

You are opening a restaurant, and tonight is opening night. Chef Mia hands you a budget of $50 per guest. Your job is to build a five-course dinner (appetizer, soup, salad, main course and dessert) and see how many stars your menu earns.

How to play

  1. Each turn, Chef Mia reveals today’s inspiration: a cuisine and an ingredient, such as Italian and lemon or Indian and mango.
  2. Pick one of the matching dishes. Every dish shows its course, its cost and its star rating.
  3. Serve it to fill that course. Courses can be filled in any order, but a served course is final.
  4. After five picks, your restaurant’s rating is the average of your five dishes, shown to two decimals.

The game always keeps enough money aside to finish every course, so the strategy is choosing when to spend on a five-star dish and when to save. A perfect 5.00 menu exists, but you will need luck to be offered it.

What’s on the menu

90 illustrated dishes from six cuisines: Italian, French, Japanese, Indian, Mexican and American, built from 25 ingredients, from butter chicken and beef bourguignon to tiramisu and strawberry daifuku. A game takes about a minute and a half, so it is easy to cook again and chase a better score.

Play anywhere

5-Star Chef Restaurant works on phones, tablets and computers with no download or sign-up. Add it to your home screen to play it like an app, even offline. When your dinner is done, share your menu and star rating with friends.
