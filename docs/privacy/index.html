Privacy

5-Star Chef Restaurant · starchefmia.com · updated 10 November 2026

You can play without an account, and the game never asks for your name, email address or phone number. This page lists what is collected while you play and why.

How the game is played (our play log)

To see how the game is used and improve it, the game records:

  • your progress in each game: courses served, dishes picked, money spent, the final score, and whether you shared your menu;
  • the kind of device (phone, tablet or computer), its operating system and screen size;
  • how you arrived: the referring website and any campaign tag on the link;
  • your approximate country, worked out by our server from the connection. Your IP address is not stored;
  • a random identifier kept in your browser, so a visit and a returning player can be recognised. It is not linked to who you are.

Leaderboards

If you choose to post a menu to a leaderboard, the restaurant name you type, your menu and its score are shown publicly. Nothing is posted unless you press Post.

Advertising measurement (Meta Pixel)

We use the Meta Pixel to measure how our ads on Facebook and Instagram perform. It sets cookies and sends Meta information about your visit and a few actions on this site: opening the page, posting to a leaderboard, and sharing a menu. Meta may combine this with other information it holds about you, as described in Meta's Privacy Policy. You can manage how Meta uses it in your Meta ad preferences, and you can block it by blocking third-party cookies or trackers in your browser.

Stored on your device

Your game in progress, settings (sound and reduced motion), your personal best and your restaurant name are kept in your browser's local storage so the game can pick up where you left off. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

Services we use

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site.
  • Supabase stores the play log and leaderboards.
  • Meta receives the advertising measurement described above.
