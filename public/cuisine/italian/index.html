Italian dinner menu game: five courses for $50

From crisp tomato bruschetta to coffee-soaked tiramisu, the Italian kitchen in 5-Star Chef Restaurant is built on tomato, lemon, mushroom and slow-cooked beef. Its best dishes are expensive: the braised beef costs almost half the budget, so an Italian five-star dinner is a lesson in where to splurge.

In 5-Star Chef Restaurant you plan a five-course dinner (appetizer, soup, salad, main course and dessert) with $50 per guest. Each turn, Chef Mia reveals a cuisine and an ingredient, you pick a matching dish, and your menu's rating is the average of your five dishes.

Can you earn five stars?
